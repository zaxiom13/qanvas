## Roadmap

1. **Installability — shipped (#28).** `public/` had only `icon.svg` while the manifest and `index.html` pointed at PNGs, so those URLs returned the HTML shell. Real PNGs are generated by `tools/make-icons.mjs`. Headless Chrome reports both PNGs as `image/png` and no installability errors. Not done: a real Android install, an iOS home-screen check, Lighthouse.
2. **Offline — spot-checked, not closed.** On #32's production build, after one online load, a cold reload with the network off opened Learn, a saved sketch, a share link, Dojo, Ref, and a malformed share hash. Precache is 21 entries (2,491.60 KiB) at `a7a6057` and includes the hashed JS, CSS and woff2 fonts. The 512 px icons are deliberately not precached. Not done: an `autoUpdate` swap mid-session over an unsaved edit.
3. **Saved work — increment shipped (#29).** Pending edits flush on sketch switch, tab switch, hide and `pagehide`, with a synchronous `localStorage` stash because a reload can drop the IndexedDB write. A quota failure keeps "saving…" and shows one toast. Not done: progress schema version, export/import backup, two-tab `markDone` races.
4. **Portrait and landscape — increment open (#30).** Safe-area padding was only on the bottom nav and the SymbolBar. #30 pads the shell, the console drawer and the fullscreen stage, shortens the landscape top bar to 44 px, and drops the Examples/Mine labels when the editor pane is under 420 px. Phone portrait and desktop Sketch/Dojo screenshots match `main` byte for byte. Not done: landscape with the soft keyboard open, a physical notch.
5. **Main-thread responsiveness — not started.** `runBudget` / `frameBudget` still don't run inside one huge primitive. A Worker stays a proposal; not started.
6. **Bundle — measured, not split.** One JS chunk: 2,151.33 kB (gzip 664.59 kB) at `a7a6057`, up from 2,149.61 kB (gzip 663.92 kB) at `083e076`. No code splitting this run.
7. **Android wrapper — parked.** Not confirmed. PWA installability is the committed goal. Do not start a TWA/WebView shell until Zak confirms or the PWA milestones above are done.

## Feature cards

### F7 Share polish — in progress

- **Shipped:** increment 1, [#31](https://github.com/zaxiom13/qanvas/pull/31), merged. Opening `#/s/…` reuses an unedited copy (optional `share` field, link format unchanged). An edited copy is left alone and a fresh one opens. A link that fails to decode shows a toast and falls back to `#/sketch`.
- **Evidence:** opening one link 3× made 3 copies before and 1 after. Editing a copy and reopening kept the edit and showed the pristine shared code. Odd names round-trip (`test/share.test.ts`, 6 tests).
- **Rough edges:** dedupe is per device and only for unedited copies. A `%`-escape cut in half never reached this code; that crash is #32.
- **Next iterations:**
  1. Measure share-link length against mobile browser and chat-app URL limits, and warn before copying a link that is likely to be truncated.
  2. Share preview on open: name plus a locally rendered thumbnail.
  3. A QR code for phone hand-off, generated locally, no remote service.
